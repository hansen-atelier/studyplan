# 27-Week "Unstoppable" Study Plan

**Start date:** Week 1, Day 1 (today)
**Subjects (11):** Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math, Malay, Chinese, English, History, Sejarah, Moral, Geography
**Fixed constraints:** School ends 4:15 pm → home by 5:00 pm

---

## 1. The Daily Skeleton (Mon–Fri)

The whole plan runs on this exact sequence, every school day. Same order, same times — no decisions to make, no room for "should I start now or later."

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 5:00–5:30 pm | **Reset** | Shower, snack, 15 min lie-down. Non-negotiable — you cannot study well on a fried brain. |
| 5:30–5:35 pm | **Launch trigger** | Phone goes in another room (or a drawer, on silent). Desk cleared. Open today's subject folder. This 5-min ritual is your "start switch." |
| 5:35–6:25 pm | **Study Block 1** (50 min) | Subject A (see rotation below) |
| 6:25–6:35 pm | Break | Stretch, water, no phone |
| 6:35–7:25 pm | **Study Block 2** (50 min) | Subject B |
| 7:25–8:00 pm | Dinner | Full break |
| 8:00–8:50 pm | **Study Block 3** (50 min) | Subject C (lighter subject) |
| 8:50–9:00 pm | **Close the loop** | Write down exactly what you'll do first tomorrow (e.g. "redo Q4–6 momentum"). This kills tomorrow's cold-start hesitation. |
| 9:00 pm onward | Free | Wind down, sleep by 10:30–11 pm |

**Total weekday study time:** 2.5 hrs/day × 5 days = 12.5 hrs/week — realistic and sustainable, not a burnout schedule.

## 2. Weekly Subject Rotation

Core subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math) get 2 blocks/week each. The other 7 subjects get 1 block/week each. That's 8 + 7 = 15 blocks, matching your 15 weekday slots exactly.

| Day | Block 1 (5:35) | Block 2 (6:35) | Block 3 (8:00, lighter) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Math | Physics | English |
| **Tue** | Chemistry | Biology | Sejarah |
| **Wed** | Math | Physics | Malay |
| **Thu** | Chemistry | Biology | Chinese |
| **Fri** | Math | Physics *(or swap w/ Chem if a test is closer)* | Moral |

Wait — that's only 5 slots for the 4th round of core subjects and misses History/Geo. Use this corrected version:

| Day | Block 1 | Block 2 | Block 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Math | Physics | English |
| **Tue** | Chemistry | Biology | History |
| **Wed** | Math | Physics | Malay |
| **Thu** | Chemistry | Biology | Sejarah |
| **Fri** | Math | Geography | Chinese + Moral (25 min each) |

**Weekend:**
- **Saturday (3 hrs, e.g. 9:30–12:30):** Past-year/exam-style questions only — rotate through Physics, Chem, Bio, Math, one per week in priority order based on your weakest topic.
- **Sunday:** 1 hour max — flashcard/formula review across all 11 subjects (10 min quick-fire skim per subject won't fit, so pick 4–5 subjects that need it that week). Everything else = real rest. Protecting Sunday rest is what prevents the burnout that causes procrastination in weeks 3–4.

Adjust the table freely as tests approach — the point is structure, not rigidity.

## 3. The 27-Week Arc

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Foundation** | 1–8 | Work through syllabus content properly, topic by topic. Build the daily habit until it's automatic — this phase is as much about training consistency as content. |
| **2. Practice** | 9–18 | Shift each block toward past-year questions and active recall (not re-reading notes). Start an **error log**: every mistake gets written down with why you got it wrong. |
| **3. Intensive Revision** | 19–24 | Full timed past-paper practice under exam conditions. Weekly review of your error log — this is where marks actually get won back. |
| **4. Final Sprint** | 25–27 | No new topics after week 26. Light review, formula sheets, past-paper skims only. Prioritize sleep. Week 27 = confidence-building, not cramming. |

## 4. Anti-Procrastination Rules

1. **The 2-minute rule:** Don't wait to "feel ready." Just open the book and do the first tiny task (e.g. read one page). Motivation follows action, not the other way round.
2. **Phone out of the room** during all three blocks — not just silenced, physically elsewhere.
3. **Same start time, every day.** The 5:30 pm trigger ritual removes the daily "when should I start" decision, which is where procrastination usually creeps in.
4. **Close the loop** every night (see step above) — never end a session not knowing what comes next.
5. **Visible tracker** — check off each completed block (table below). Seeing an unbroken chain is a strong motivator; don't break the chain.
6. **One rest day is protected, not earned.** Sunday afternoon rest happens regardless of how the week went — this prevents the "I'm behind, why bother" spiral.
7. **If you miss a block, don't double up next time** — just resume the rotation. Doubling up is what makes the plan feel punishing and causes people to quit it.
